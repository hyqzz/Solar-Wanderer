# Negative Comment / Crisis Response Template

## Response principles

1. **Acknowledge first.** Don't argue.
2. **Apologize if warranted.** Even if it's a misunderstanding.
3. **Explain briefly.** Provide context without being defensive.
4. **Offer a fix or next step.** Invite to GitHub issue, DM, or email.
5. **Take it private if needed.** Don't escalate publicly.

---

## Common scenarios

### 1. "It's too slow / crashes on my phone"

**English:**

> Sorry it didn't run smoothly for you, [name]. Performance varies by device and browser. Could you let us know your device + browser? We're actively optimizing and tracking issues here: https://github.com/hyqzz/Solar-Wanderer/issues

**中文：**

> 抱歉在你手机上运行不够流畅 [名字]。性能会因设备和浏览器差异较大。能否告诉我们你的手机型号和浏览器？我们正在持续优化，也可以在这里提 issue：https://github.com/hyqzz/Solar-Wanderer/issues

---

### 2. "The data isn't real / planets look wrong"

**English:**

> The positions are computed from real NASA JPL ephemerides, but visual scale is sometimes adjusted so you can actually see things (e.g., planet sizes are exaggerated at extreme zoom). If something looks off, please open an issue with a screenshot and we'll investigate: https://github.com/hyqzz/Solar-Wanderer/issues/new

**中文：**

> 位置是基于 NASA JPL 星历真实计算的。但为了让画面可读，部分视觉尺度做了调整（比如远距离时行星大小会夸张显示）。如果你发现明显异常，欢迎带截图开 issue：https://github.com/hyqzz/Solar-Wanderer/issues/new

---

### 3. "What's the point? We have NASA Eyes"

**English:**

> NASA Eyes is excellent. Solar Wanderer is browser-based, open source, and focused on true 1:1 scale exploration from surface to Oort Cloud with no install. Different tools for different needs — we hope people find both useful.

**中文：**

> NASA Eyes 确实很优秀。遨游太阳系的优势是浏览器直接打开、开源、强调从地表到奥尔特云的真实 1:1 尺度探索，无需安装。两者定位不同，希望大家都能用到适合自己的工具。

---

### 4. "This is just another Three.js demo"

**English:**

> Fair take — the visuals are built with Three.js, but the core is the ephemeris math and floating-origin rendering that lets us maintain 1:1 km scale from a moon's surface to 100,000 AU. The code is open if you'd like to dig in: https://github.com/hyqzz/Solar-Wanderer

**中文：**

> 说得没错，渲染层用 Three.js，但核心是我们自己写的星历计算和浮动原点渲染，能在真实 1:1 km 尺度下从卫星表面支撑到 10 万 AU。代码完全开源，欢迎查看：https://github.com/hyqzz/Solar-Wanderer

---

### 5. Accusation / hostile comment

**English:**

> Sorry you feel that way. If there's a specific issue we can help with, please email [your email] or open an issue. We're here to improve.

**中文：**

> 抱歉让你有不好的体验。如果有具体问题，可以发邮件到 [your email] 或开 issue，我们会认真对待。

---

## Do NOT do

- Don't delete criticism unless it's spam/hate speech.
- Don't argue publicly over taste.
- Don't ignore bug reports.
- Don't promise timelines you can't keep.

## Escalation

If a comment becomes harassment or misinformation,:
1. Screenshot it.
2. Respond once calmly.
3. Take the conversation to DM/email.
4. Block/report if necessary.
