## 推文 #2（3 小时后发，配截图）

The solar system is bigger than any game lets you feel.

Solar Wanderer renders it at true 1:1 scale — from 0.5 m above a surface to 100,000 AU at the Oort Cloud.

Planetary positions are verified against NASA JPL Horizons.

Open source. MIT. Free forever.

→ https://github.com/hyqzz/Solar-Wanderer
