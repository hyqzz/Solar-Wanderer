## 正文

What if anyone, on any device, could stand on the Moon and watch Earth rise?

That’s the idea behind Solar Wanderer, a browser-based 1:1 real-time solar system explorer I’ve been building. It’s powered by NASA JPL ephemerides, meaning the planets are exactly where they should be right now.

You can:

- Zoom from orbit to the surface of the Moon, Mars, Europa, Titan, Pluto, and 14 other worlds.
- Walk around with real surface gravity.
- Fly through Saturn’s rings and pull back to the Oort Cloud.
- Verify every position against JPL Horizons.

No install. No account. No backend. Just a ~200 KB web page.

I built this because I believe the best way to prepare humanity for the stars is to first help us truly see our own backyard.

If you’re an educator, a parent, or just someone who looks up at the night sky and wonders, I’d love for you to try it.

→ https://sw.icodestar.net

Source: https://github.com/hyqzz/Solar-Wanderer
