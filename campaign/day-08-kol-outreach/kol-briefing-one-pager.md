# KOL Briefing One-Pager

## Solar Wanderer · 遨游太阳系

**One line:** Free browser app that lets anyone explore the real solar system at true 1:1 scale.

---

## Key facts

- **Live demo:** https://sw.icodestar.net
- **GitHub:** https://github.com/hyqzz/Solar-Wanderer
- **License:** MIT, open source, no ads, no registration
- **Tech:** Three.js + Vite + WebGL2, NASA JPL ephemerides
- **Accuracy:** Planets ≤0.1°, Moon ≈0.12°, major moons ≤0.22° vs JPL Horizons
- **Scale:** 0.5 m to 100,000 AU
- **Bodies:** Sun, 8 planets, 21 major moons, rings, asteroid belt, Kuiper Belt, Oort Cloud
- **Modes:** Orbit/explore, free flight, surface walk, underwater

---

## Best on-camera moments

1. **Earthrise on the Moon** — land on the Moon, look up, watch Earth rise.
2. **Mars blue sunset** — land on Mars at sunset, see the blue hue.
3. **Saturn ring drift** — fly slowly through the rings.
4. **Jupiter storm approach** — descend toward the Great Red Spot.
5. **Scale pull-back** — start on a moon surface, zoom out to see the whole solar system.
6. **Real-time clock** — show that positions match the current real date.

---

## Suggested talking points

- This isn't an animation; positions are computed from real orbital data.
- It runs in a phone browser, no install.
- It's open source, so anyone can contribute.
- The mission: give people a real sense of where we are in space before we actually leave Earth.
