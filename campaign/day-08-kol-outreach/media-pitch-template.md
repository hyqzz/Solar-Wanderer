## Body

Hi [Editor/Journalist name],

Quick pitch: a solo developer just shipped **Solar Wanderer** — a browser-based, real-time, 1:1 scale model of the solar system built on NASA JPL ephemerides.

**Why it matters:**

- No install, no sign-up, no ads. Works on phone and desktop.
- Users can drop from orbit to the surface of the Moon, Mars, Io, Titan, and 15 other solid bodies.
- Positions are accurate to within ~0.1° compared to NASA JPL Horizons.
- The camera can zoom from 0.5 m above a moon's surface to 100,000 AU at the Oort Cloud.
- Open source under MIT license.
