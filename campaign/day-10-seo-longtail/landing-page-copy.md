## 页面 1：太阳系模拟器 / Solar System Simulator

### H1
Solar Wanderer — A Real-Time, 1:1 Scale Solar System Simulator in Your Browser

### 副标题
No install. No sign-up. Just open your browser and explore the real solar system, from the Sun to the Oort Cloud, using NASA JPL ephemerides.

### 正文

Most solar system apps are cartoons. Solar Wanderer is different: every planet, moon, and ring is placed using real orbital data from NASA JPL. The scale is true 1:1. You can stand on the Moon and watch Earth rise. You can fly to Mars and see the blue sunset. You can drift through Saturn's rings and zoom all the way out to 100,000 AU.

**What makes it accurate:**

- Planetary positions match JPL Horizons within ~0.1°.
- The Moon is positioned using a truncated ELP model.
- 21 major moons are fitted to JPL state vectors.
- Time is computed in Terrestrial Time (TT) and Julian Date.

**What you can do:**

- Land on 19 solid bodies.
- Switch between orbit camera, free flight, and surface walk.
- Toggle inertial / body-fixed anchoring.
- Watch positions update in real time.

## 页面 2：月球表面模拟器 / Moon Surface Simulator

### H1
Stand on the Moon and Watch Earth Rise — Free Moon Surface Simulator

### 副标题
Solar Wanderer lets you land on the Moon in your browser. Real terrain, real lighting, real Earth position.

### 正文

The Moon is only 384,400 km away, but most people have never seen what Earthrise looks like from the surface. Solar Wanderer changes that.

Open the app, focus the Moon, and descend. When you land, you'll see craters, hills, and the Earth hanging in the black sky — at the correct phase and position for the current date and time.

**Features:**

- Real Moon terrain generated with procedural detail.
- Earth phase matches real illumination.
- Walk on the surface with WASD.
- Surface gravity modeled at 1.62 m/s².

**Why it's useful:**

- Education: show students the Earth-Moon system from a new perspective.
- Curiosity: finally see Earthrise for yourself.
- Content: capture screenshots and videos for social media.

## 页面 3：火星探索模拟器 / Mars Exploration Simulator

### H1
Explore Mars in Your Browser — Free Mars Exploration Simulator

### 副标题
Fly to Mars, land on the surface, and see the famous blue sunset. Real NASA data, real scale, no install.

### 正文

Mars has fascinated humans for centuries. Solar Wanderer lets you go there in seconds.

Focus Mars, accelerate time, and watch its orbit. Descend through the thin atmosphere and land on rust-colored terrain. Wait until sunset and you'll see why Mars sunsets look blue: fine dust scatters blue light forward toward the observer.

**What you can explore:**

- Olympus Mons and Valles Marineris regions.
- Real Martian day length (24h 37m).
- Phobos and Deimos orbiting overhead.
- Accurate position relative to Earth and Sun.

**Use cases:**

- Classroom demonstrations.
- Science communication.
- Personal space exploration.

[Fly to Mars — https://sw.icodestar.net]
