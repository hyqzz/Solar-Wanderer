# User Story Collection Template

## Prompt (English)

**Tell us your Solar Wanderer story.**

How did you find the project? What did you explore first? What surprised you? How did it change how you think about space?

Share your story and we may feature it on our website, README, or social channels.

**Format:**

- Name / handle:
- Location:
- Background (student, teacher, developer, etc.):
- First thing you explored:
- Most surprising moment:
- How you'd use it:
- Quote we can share:
- Links (optional):

## Example story

> "I opened Solar Wanderer during my lunch break and landed on the Moon. When I looked up and saw Earth rising over the crater rim, I stopped eating. I'd seen the photo a hundred times, but this felt like I was actually there. Now I show it to my students every semester."
> — Sarah, high-school physics teacher, USA

> "我用午休时间打开遨游太阳系，降落在月球上。抬头看到地球从环形山边缘升起时，我愣住了。那张照片我见过无数次，但这一次我感觉自己真的站在那里。现在我每学期都会给学生展示。"
> — Sarah，美国高中物理老师
