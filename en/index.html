Controls (H to close)

🌐 Explore mode (default · Google Earth style)

Left-dragOrbit around focus (with inertia, slows near ground)
Ctrl+Left-dragRotate heading / tilt (3D look-around)
Right/Middle-dragPan the whole space (no rotation, R resets)
W S A D / ArrowsPan view (N/S/W/E, speed scales with altitude)
Shift+A / Shift+DRotate heading (CCW / CW)
Shift+W / Shift+STilt toward horizon / back to top-down
Click a bodyLock focus (wheel then approaches/retreats toward it, down to landing)
Wheel / PageUp PageDown / + −Zoom toward screen center (altitude-proportional: meter steps at ground, hover at any altitude; gentle = fine, repeated/held = accelerates)
Wheel all the way inSeamless surface landing (auto-switches to walk); oceans land on water
VInertial view mode (camera does not co-rotate · pair with time warp to watch moons orbit)
RReset view (north up, top-down)
🪐 Body directoryClick a body → fly-to animation (left side, expanded by default)
Double-click label / T / 1–9,0GE-style fly-to (target centered)
Drag (mid-flight)Interrupt fly-to animation
GLand and walk when near surface
FToggle free-flight mode

🚶 Surface walking

Mouse / W S A DView (360° free look) / walk (true surface gravity)
Wheel back / PageUpSeamless takeoff back to explore mode (view direction kept)
Wheel forward on water / PageDownDive — low-sensitivity wheel ascent/descent, hover when stopped (hold any depth), auto-resurface
Shift / SpaceRun / jump (6× higher on the Moon); Space ascends underwater
GBack to explore mode

🚀 Free flight (F to enter)

W S A D R FSix-direction translation
Wheel / ShiftSpeed tier (1 m/s → 2 AU/s) / boost
XFull stop (hover anywhere)
EscBack to explore mode
