🛰️ 关于土卫一 / About Mimas

巨大的赫歇尔撞击坑使它酷似“死星”。

Mimas (土卫一) is a moon orbiting Saturn with a radius of 198.2 km and surface gravity of 0.06 m/s². You can land on and walk on Mimas in Solar Wanderer. Explore Mimas in real-time 1:1 scale at Solar Wanderer.
